Sleazeburg 64

A top-down crime satire for the Commodore 64, written in 6502 assembly: the real-hardware version of the browser game of the same name. This is an early build: a car to drive around the city, with the view scrolling smoothly to follow it. No traffic, people or game yet.

Play with the keyboard Play on a phone or tablet Download the .prg

Controls

The joystick in port 2: up accelerates, down brakes and then reverses, left and right steer, and fire is the handbrake. In the browser the cursor keys are the stick and space is fire; on a phone there is a joystick on the screen.

On a real C64 or C128

Copy sleazeburg64.prg to a disk, SD card or cartridge, plug a joystick into port 2, then LOAD"*",8,1 (or its name in place of the star) and RUN. It is written for PAL machines.

About

The browser version runs in VirtualC64 Web (GPL-3.0), an emulator on its own site, with the open-source MEGA65 Open ROMs (LGPL-3.0) in place of Commodore's.
